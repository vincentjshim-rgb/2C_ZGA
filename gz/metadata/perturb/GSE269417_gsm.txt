Briefly, the tubes containing single embryos in 100 µL RNA-Bee were thawed and vortexed and 20 µL of chloroform was added. After vortexing rigorously for 15-30 seconds, the tubes were placed on ice for 5 min and then centrifuged at 12,000 g for 15 min at 4°C. The aqueous phase was transferred into a new 1.5 mL tube containing 3 µL of 0.25% linear polyacrylamide which was used as a carrier. 50 µL of isopropanol was added and vortexed for 15 sec, and the samples were kept on ice for 30 minutes to precipitate the RNA. After centrifugation at 10,000g for 15 min at 4°C the pellet of RNA was washed with 0.4 mL of cold 75% ethanol. After re-spinning and brief air drying, the total RNA was dissolved in 22.3 µL of 10 mM Tris-HCl buffer pH 8.5 containing 0.2 U/µL RNasin® ribonuclease inhibitor (Promega, Cat. N2515). Potential genomic DNA contamination was removed from the total RNA using 0.2 µL of rDNase I from the DNA-free Kit (Ambion, Cat. AM1906). After the DNase Inactivation Reagent (0.1 volume) was added and centrifuged, the RNA was transferred into a new tube containing 3 µL of 0.25% linear polyacrylamide and recovered by ethanol precipitation.  The single embryo total RNA samples were eluted in 7 µL of 10 mM Tris-HCl buffer pH 8.5 containing 0.2 U/µL RNasin® ribonuclease inhibitor.
