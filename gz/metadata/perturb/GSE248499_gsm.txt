The cumulus-free oocytes were enucleated in Hepes-buffered KSOM containing 7.5 μg/mL cytochalasin B using a Piezo-driven pipette. After being cultured in fresh KSOM for 1 h, the enucleated oocytes were injected with cumulus cell nuclei in Hepes-buffered KSOM at room temperature. !Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = C57BL/6N female mice at 4–20 weeks of age were superovulated by injection of 7.5 IU equine chorionic gonadotropin  or anti-inhibin serum, followed 46–52 h later by injection of 7.5 IU human chorionic gonadotropin. Female mice were euthanized by cervical dislocation 16 h after the hCG injection, and then cumulus–oocyte complexes were collected from the ampullae of excised oviducts 16 h after the hCG injection, and placed in 100-μL droplets of human tubal fluid medium. For in vitro fertilization, spermatozoa were collected from the cauda epididymidis of adult DBA2 male mice and preincubated for at least 30 min in a 400-μL droplet of HTF. After preincubation, 3–5 μL of the sperm suspension was added to the oocyte cultures. At 3 h post-insemination, morphologically normal fertilized embryos were collected and cultured in potassium simplex optimized medium. For somatic cell nuclear transfer, cumulus cells used as nuclear donors were collected from wild-type female mice with a BDF1 (C57BL/6N ×DBA2) genetic background. Recipient oocytes were collected from BDF1 female mice following superovulation as above, and placed in KSOM droplets containing 0.1% bovine testicular hyaluronidase to remove cumulus cells. The cumulus-free oocytes were enucleated in Hepes-buffered KSOM containing 7.5 μg/mL cytochalasin B using a Piezo-driven pipette. After being cultured in fresh KSOM for 1 h, the enucleated oocytes were injected with cumulus cell nuclei in Hepes-buffered KSOM at room temperature. After 1 h culture, the injected oocytes were activated by culture in a Ca2+-free KSOM containing 3 mM strontium chloride and 5 μg/mL cytochalasin B for 1 h. The reconstructed oocytes were cultured in KSOM containing 5 μg/mL cytochalasin B for 5 h. After being washed thoroughly, the embryos were further cultured in KSOM.

!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = C57BL/6N female mice at 4–20 weeks of age were superovulated by injection of 7.5 IU equine chorionic gonadotropin  or anti-inhibin serum, followed 46–52 h later by injection of 7.5 IU human chorionic gonadotropin. Female mice were euthanized by cervical dislocation 16 h after the hCG injection, and then cumulus–oocyte complexes were collected from the ampullae of excised oviducts 16 h after the hCG injection, and placed in 100-μL droplets of human tubal fluid medium. For in vitro fertilization, spermatozoa were collected from the cauda epididymidis of adult DBA2 male mice and preincubated for at least 30 min in a 400-μL droplet of HTF. After preincubation, 3–5 μL of the sperm suspension was added to the oocyte cultures. At 3 h post-insemination, morphologically normal fertilized embryos were collected and cultured in potassium simplex optimized medium. For somatic cell nuclear transfer, cumulus cells used as nuclear donors were collected from wild-type female mice with a BDF1 (C57BL/6N ×DBA2) genetic background. Recipient oocytes were collected from BDF1 female mice following superovulation as above, and placed in KSOM droplets containing 0.1% bovine testicular hyaluronidase to remove cumulus cells. The cumulus-free oocytes were enucleated in Hepes-buffered KSOM containing 7.5 μg/mL cytochalasin B using a Piezo-driven pipette. After being cultured in fresh KSOM for 1 h, the enucleated oocytes were injected with cumulus cell nuclei in Hepes-buffered KSOM at room temperature. After 1 h culture, the injected oocytes were activated by culture in a Ca2+-free KSOM containing 3 mM strontium chloride and 5 μg/mL cytochalasin B for 1 h. The reconstructed oocytes were cultured in KSOM containing 5 μg/mL cytochalasin B for 5 h. After being washed thoroughly, the embryos were further cultured in KSOM.

!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = C57BL/6N female mice at 4–20 weeks of age were superovulated by injection of 7.5 IU equine chorionic gonadotropin  or anti-inhibin serum, followed 46–52 h later by injection of 7.5 IU human chorionic gonadotropin. Female mice were euthanized by cervical dislocation 16 h after the hCG injection, and then cumulus–oocyte complexes were collected from the ampullae of excised oviducts 16 h after the hCG injection, and placed in 100-μL droplets of human tubal fluid medium. For in vitro fertilization, spermatozoa were collected from the cauda epididymidis of adult DBA2 male mice and preincubated for at least 30 min in a 400-μL droplet of HTF. After preincubation, 3–5 μL of the sperm suspension was added to the oocyte cultures. At 3 h post-insemination, morphologically normal fertilized embryos were collected and cultured in potassium simplex optimized medium. For somatic cell nuclear transfer, cumulus cells used as nuclear donors were collected from wild-type female mice with a BDF1 (C57BL/6N ×DBA2) genetic background. Recipient oocytes were collected from BDF1 female mice following superovulation as above, and placed in KSOM droplets containing 0.1% bovine testicular hyaluronidase to remove cumulus cells. The cumulus-free oocytes were enucleated in Hepes-buffered KSOM containing 7.5 μg/mL cytochalasin B using a Piezo-driven pipette. After being cultured in fresh KSOM for 1 h, the enucleated oocytes were injected with cumulus cell nuclei in Hepes-buffered KSOM at room temperature. After 1 h culture, the injected oocytes were activated by culture in a Ca2+-free KSOM containing 3 mM strontium chloride and 5 μg/mL cytochalasin B for 1 h. The reconstructed oocytes were cultured in KSOM containing 5 μg/mL cytochalasin B for 5 h. After being washed thoroughly, the embryos were further cultured in KSOM.
