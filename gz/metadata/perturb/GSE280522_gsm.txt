In the control group, an equivalent amount of DMSO (0.1%) was added. !Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = A485 (Selleck, S8740) dissolved in dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) (Sigma-Aldrich, D2650) was added to KSOM at a final concentration of 1 μM. To transiently inhibit p300/CBP in minor ZGA, embryos were cultured in A485 during post-hCG injection: 22 h~37 h. Embryos were washed at least three times in KSOM to remove A485. In the control group, an equivalent amount of DMSO (0.1%) was added. For DUX rescue embryos, Dux-Flag mRNA (15 ng/μl or 100 ng/μl) was injected into cytoplasm of M1C, E2C and M2C embryos (23 h, 37 h and 42h post hCG injection) with a Piezo-drill (Eppendorf) and Eppendorf Transferman 4r micromanipulators. Samples were injected at 5–10 pl per zygote or 2C embryo.
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Female BDF1 mice (C57BL/6 × DBA2, Beijing Vital River Laboratory Animal Technology), aged 8-10 weeks, were super-ovulated with an injection of 7.5 IU of pregnant mare’s serum gonadotropin (PMSG) (San-Sheng Pharmaceutical, 110914564), followed by an injection of 7.5 IU of human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) (San-Sheng Pharmaceutical, 110911282) 48 hours later. After hCG injection, females were mated with BDF1 stud male mice. Zygotes were collected from the oviduct 20 hours post-hCG, and the surrounding cumulus cells were removed using hyaluronidase (Sigma-Aldrich, H4272). Embryos were cultured in KSOM (Millipore, MR-016D) and incubated at 37°C with 5% CO2 in humidified air.

!Sample_data_processing = RNA-seq reads were aligned to the mouse genome (mm10) with Hisat2 (version 2.2.1)(Kim et al., 2015) after removing adaptor sequences and low-quality reads by Trim_Galore(version 4.0; https://www.bioinformatics.babraham.ac.uk/projects/trim_galore/). The read counts and normalized FPKM values for each gene were calculated with featureCounts (version 2.0.1)(Kim et al., 2015) and cufflinks (version 2.2.1)(Trapnell et al., 2010). To quantify the repetitive sequences, TEtranscripts (version 2.2.3)(Jin et al., 2015) was used to calculate the read counts based on RepeatMasker annotation from the UCSC genome annotation database. To calculate the read counts of Obox4, trimmed sequencing reads were also mapped to the reference Obox4 mRNA sequence by Magic-BLAST(Boratyn et al., 2019). The read counts were normalized by total reads and gene length for estimation of FPKM.
